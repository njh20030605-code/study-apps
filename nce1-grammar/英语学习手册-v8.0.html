判断口诀 · 三秒决定用哪个
① 看位置：在动词前面当主语 → 主格 (he / she / they / I / we)
② 看位置：在动词后面 / 介词后面当宾语 → 宾格 (him / her / them / me / us)
③ 介词包括 to / for / with / from / about / at 等。比如 give it to him（给他）、go with us（跟我们一起）。

名词性物主代词为什么存在？—— 避免重复。
✗ This is my book and that is her book. （重复 book）
✅ This book is mine and that one is hers.

be 动词系统
最核心的骨架

be 动词的三种形态

I → am
只有"我"用 am
he / she / it → is
单数第三人称用 is
we / you / they → are
复数 + you 用 are
+ 名词复数 → are
Tom and Jack are friends.
过去式 → was / were
am/is → was，are → were（第 37 课之后会详细学）· 原形都是 be，这就是 "be 动词" 名字的由来

陈述句 → be 动词疑问句

Is / Am / Are + 主语 + 其余部分 ?
This is a book.
→ Is this a book?
把 is 提到最前，其余不动
They are students.
→ Are they students?
复数用 are，同样提到最前
She is a teacher.
→ Is she a teacher?
she / he / it 用 is

陈述句 → be 动词否定句

主语 + am / is / are + not + 其余部分
I am a student.
→ I am not a student.
am 后面直接加 not，无缩写形式 ⚠️
He is happy.
→ He is not happy. / He isn't happy.
is + not = isn't（可缩写）
They are here.
→ They are not here. / They aren't here.
are + not = aren't（可缩写）
⚠️ I'm not — 唯一的缩写方式
I am not 只能缩写成 I'm not，不能说 I amn't（不存在）。
· ✅ I'm not a doctor.   ✅ I am not a doctor.
· ✗ I amn't a doctor.

PART 03
一般现在时
描述习惯和事实
⚡
CORE RULE
每个英语句子必须有且只有一个谓语动词。
检查句子的第一步：找动词。没有动词 = 不完整；两个动词 = 连接词出了问题。

第三人称单数 · 动词加 s / es

he / she / it + 动词 + s / es （表习惯动作）
She go to work. ✗
→ She goes to work.
goes = go + es，表示习惯动作，不是过去式
He work every day. ✗
→ He works every day.
work → works，主语是 he
（过去某一天发生）
→ She went to work.
went = go 的过去式（不规则），表示已发生

Do vs Does · 怎么选

DO
I · you · we · they
（复数 / 我 / 你）
Do you like coffee?
Do they have a cat?
DOES
he · she · it
（第三人称单数）
Does he have a car?
Does she go to school?
记住：does 只服务 he / she / it，其他全用 do。而且 does 出现之后，后面的动词必须回到原形——比如 has → have、goes → go。

否定句：两条路 · be 动词 vs 普通动词

英语否定句只有两条路，看句子里的动词是 be 还是普通动词，选错路就全错。
be 动词否定
is / are 直接加 not
→ isn't / aren't
He is tall. → He isn't tall.
They are lazy. → They aren't lazy.
普通动词否定
必须借 don't / doesn't
+ 动词原形
He speaks English.
→ He doesn't speak English.
They work hard.
→ They don't work hard.
don't vs doesn't · 只看主语
doesn't → he / she / it（第三人称单数）
don't → I / you / we / they（其余全部）
名词主语怎么判断？— 看它等于 he/she/it 还是 they
名词主语 等于 用 否定句举例
the weather= itdoesn't The weather doesn't change.
my mother= shedoesn't My mother doesn't cook dinner.
the car= itdoesn't The car doesn't go fast.
Tom= hedoesn't Tom doesn't eat meat.
the sun= itdoesn't The sun doesn't shine.
his father= hedoesn't His father doesn't wash the dishes.
the students= theydon't The students don't ask questions.
the dogs= theydon't The dogs don't eat cakes.
my children= theydon't My children don't like lamb.
⚠️ doesn't 后面动词去掉 -s — 原形！
doesn't 已经帮主语"背"了第三人称，动词不用再加 -s。
· ✗ He doesn't speaks English.  
· ✅ He doesn't speak English.
· ✗ She doesn't cooks dinner.  
· ✅ She doesn't cook dinner.

名词不受影响，该复数还是复数：
· ✅ She doesn't sell cakes.  （原句 cakes，否定句还是 cakes）
🔑 怎么判断用 isn't 还是 doesn't？
看句子里有没有 is / am / are：

有 is/are → 直接变 isn't / aren't，不用 doesn't
· My name is John. → My name isn't John.
· The weather is cold. → The weather isn't cold.
· They are hungry. → They aren't hungry.

没有 is/are（普通动词）→ 用 don't / doesn't + 原形
· He speaks English. → He doesn't speak English.
· I like coffee. → I don't like coffee.

两条路绝对不能混！
· ✗ The weather doesn't is cold.（be 动词不能用 doesn't）
· ✗ It's doesn't windy.（It's 就是 be 动词，用 isn't）
· ✗ He not speaks English.（普通动词不能直接加 not）

陈述句 → have / has 疑问句

Do / Does + 主语 + 动词原形 + 其余 ?
He has a car.
→ Does he have a car?
has → have（does 出现后动词回原形）
They have a dog.
→ Do they have a dog?
they 是复数，用 do
She has a bag.
→ Does she have a bag?
she 单数用 does，have 不加 s

易混点 · Common Confusions

1. in vs into
in 描述状态，into 描述动作。判断方法：句子里的动词是静态的（is, stands, sits）还是动态的（jump, run, go, put）？动态动词后接 into / out of / onto。

· The cat is in the box.（静态）
· The cat jumps into the box.（动态）
2. next to = beside
这两个是同义词。next to 更口语，beside 稍正式。新概念 1 两个都会出现。
3. near vs next to
next to = 紧挨着，之间没缝隙。near = 在附近，有一点距离。

· The bank is next to the post office.（银行紧挨着邮局）
· The bank is near my home.（银行在我家附近，走 5 分钟）
4. "go off" 不是空间介词
go off 是短语动词，跟空间位置无关。常见意思：

· The alarm goes off at 7. 闹钟在 7 点响。
· He went off to Beijing. 他动身去北京了。
· The milk has gone off. 牛奶变质了。

看到 "go + 介词" 要分情况：go into / go out of 是空间移动；go off / go on / go away 是固定短语，意思要单独记。

变换规律 · 注释

易混点 · Common Confusions

1. some / any 后面：可数复数 OR 不可数都行
这两个量词不挑可数 / 不可数，但可数名词必须用复数形式。

· some bread ✅   some books ✅   some book ✗（可数单数不行）
· any water ✅   any apples ✅   any apple ✗
2. 不可数名词永远配单数动词
不管前面有没有 some，不可数名词后面的 be 动词永远是 is，行为动词永远不加 s。

· Water are cold. ✗   →   ✅ Water is cold.
· The news are bad. ✗   →   ✅ The news is bad.
· Some milk are in the fridge. ✗   →   ✅ Some milk is in the fridge.
3. a / an 只能配可数单数 · 不可数前面绝对不能加
这是 NCE1 学生最常踩的坑。看到液体、食物整体、抽象名词，先想"它能数吗？"

· a water ✗   →   ✅ some water / a glass of water
· a bread ✗   →   ✅ some bread / a piece of bread
· a money ✗   →   ✅ some money / 1000 yuan
4. There is / There are · 跟 some / any 高频组合
"有 / 存在" 句型最常和 some / any 配。判断方法看后面名词：

· There is + 不可数 / 可数单数：There is some milk. / There is a book.
· There are + 可数复数：There are some apples.
· 否定：There isn't any milk. / There aren't any apples.
· 疑问：Is there any milk? / Are there any apples?
5. 中英思维差 · 中文"一杯水"≠ 英文 a water
中文里的"一杯 / 一块 / 一片"是量词，但英文里 water / bread / cheese 这些不能直接加 a。必须先选容器词（glass、piece、slice），再用 a + 容器 + of + 名词 的结构。

这一步是从中文思维切到英文思维的关键 —— 看到液体 / 整块食物，先停一秒，想容器词。

时态主动被动 · be + p.p.
一般现在时 He writes a letter. A letter is written by him.

从原稿到改进稿 · 看懂每处为什么改

易错改写对比

中式表达 → 地道说法 · 这些坑最常踩
✗ He's very speed in every race.
✓ He runs very fast in every race.
→ speed 是名词，“跑得快”要用 run fast（动词 + 副词）
✗ I want to listen to her about Brazil.
✓ I want to hear her talk about Brazil.
→ listen to sb 只是“听某人”，“听某人讲某事”要 hear sb talk about
✗ I spent 50 mins arriving at my company.
✓ It takes me 50 mins to get to my company.
→ 花时间做某事用 it takes sb time to do，不是 spend + arriving
✗ I woke up at 7.50 O'clock.
✓ I wake up at 7:50.
→ 日常习惯用现在时；O'clock 只跟整点，7:50 不加 o'clock

Topic 1 · 想见的人（Suzi）

原稿 → 改进稿 · 对比看提升
原始版本 Before
I want to meet Suzi because I want to listen to her about Brazil. I'm a big fan of the Brazilian National Team. My idol is Neymar. He is my favourite football player. I want to ask Suzi to talk more about football. I like Brazil.
改进版本 After
I would like to meet Suzi because I'm very interested in Brazil, and she is from there. Also, I'm a big fan of the Brazilian National Team. Neymar is my favourite player in the team. Therefore, getting to know Suzi will open my eyes to Brazil and football.
改写要点
· I want to → I would like to（更礼貌）
· listen to her about → hear her talk about
· 用 Therefore 连接结论句，提升逻辑性
· open my eyes to ... = 让我大开眼界（加分表达）

Topic 2 · 写一个你认识的人（Ronaldo）

形容词清单 → 成稿 → 改写要点
人物：Ronaldo  ·  形容词清单：tall, strong, handsome, disciplined, energetic, outgoing  ·  名词搭配：willingness（意志力 / 决心）
成稿 Final
I'm going to talk about Ronaldo. He is one of the most famous football players in the world. Ronaldo is tall, strong, and handsome, but I admire him more because he is a highly disciplined person — he goes to the gym every day for hours. He also has a strong willingness to win. As an athlete, he is surely energetic, and his personality is outgoing. When he plays football, his running speed is much faster than others'. I really see him as my idol.
改写要点
· goes to gym everyday → goes to the gym every day（gym 前加 the；every day 两个词）
· his personal personality 重复 → his personality
· his speed of running is so much faster → his running speed is much faster than others'

Topic 3 · My Day（日常作息）

全程用现在一般时 · 描述一天的固定作息
Morning
I wake up at 7:50 in the morning. First, I usually brush my teeth and wash my face. I always like to put on black clothes, and I often wear perfume when I go out. At 8 o'clock, I take the metro (Line 15) to go to work. It takes me 50 minutes to get there. On the metro, I learn English every day — I don't want to waste my time.
At Work
I arrive at my company at 9 o'clock. The first thing I do is clock in. Then I have my breakfast and start working until 12. During lunch break, I have lunch in the canteen until 1 p.m. Usually, if I have relatively simple work in the afternoon, I only need to stay until 6 p.m. I usually don't have to work overtime, so I go home at 6.
Opinion about Overtime
I think it's not wise or necessary to work overtime if you can finish everything efficiently on time. I disagree with staying overtime just for some compliments from the leaders. Honestly, I really hate meaningless overtime work — I detest formalism.
Evening
I get home around 7 o'clock. First, I take off my shoes and put on my slippers, then wash my hands. We have dinner, which usually finishes around 8. After that, I study English until 9. Then I take a bath, lie down on the bed, play with my phone for a while, and go to sleep.
改写要点
· Daily routine 全部用现在一般时（wake, take, have），不要用 woke / arrived
· 7.50 O'clock → 7:50（O'clock 只跟整点）
· 50 mins → 50 minutes（正式写作不缩写）
· wash my body → take a bath / take a shower（更地道）
· play with the phone → play on / with my phone
